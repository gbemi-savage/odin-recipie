Sushi Recipe

Sushi is a Japanese dish made with seasoned rice and ingredients such as fresh fish, vegetables, and seaweed. It's delicious, customizable, and fun to make at home.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups sushi rice
  • 2½ cups water
  • ¼ cup rice vinegar
  • 1 tablespoon sugar
  • ½ teaspoon salt
  • 4 sheets nori (seaweed)
  • 200 g fresh sushi-grade salmon or tuna
  • 1 cucumber
  • 1 avocado
  • Soy sauce, for serving
  • Wasabi, optional
  • Pickled ginger, for serving

Instructions

Rinse the sushi rice until the water runs mostly clear.

Cook the rice according to the package instructions.

Mix rice vinegar, sugar, and salt. Stir into the warm rice and allow it to cool.

Place a sheet of nori on a bamboo sushi mat.

Spread a thin layer of rice over the nori, leaving a small space at the top.

Add strips of fish, cucumber, and avocado across the center.

Roll the sushi tightly using the bamboo mat.

Use a sharp, wet knife to cut the roll into pieces.

Serve with soy sauce, wasabi, and pickled ginger.

Tacos

Description

These easy homemade tacos are filled with seasoned ground beef, fresh vegetables, cheese, and a flavorful combination of toppings. They're quick to prepare and perfect for a casual dinner.

Ingredients

  • 500 g ground beef
  • 8 taco shells or tortillas
  • 1 small onion, diced
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 tablespoon cooking oil
  • 1 tablespoon chili powder
  • 1 teaspoon ground cumin
  • ½ teaspoon paprika
  • Salt and black pepper, to taste
  • 1 cup shredded lettuce
  • 1 tomato, diced
  • 1 cup shredded cheddar cheese
  • Sour cream, for serving
  • Salsa, for serving

Instructions

Heat the oil in a large frying pan over medium heat.

Add the onion and cook until softened.

Add the ground beef and cook until browned, breaking it apart as it cooks.

Add the garlic, chili powder, cumin, paprika, salt, and pepper.

Stir everything together and cook for another 2-3 minutes.

Warm the taco shells or tortillas according to their package instructions.

Fill each shell with the seasoned beef.

Add lettuce, tomato, and shredded cheese.

Top with salsa and sour cream.

Serve immediately and enjoy!
