Classic Homemade Lasagna

Description: A delicious, hearty lasagna made with layers of tender pasta, seasoned ground beef, rich tomato sauce, creamy cheese, and melted mozzarella. It's perfect for family dinners and special occasions.

Ingredients

  • 1 lb (450 g) ground beef
  • 1 medium onion, chopped
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 24 oz (680 g) tomato pasta sauce
  • 9 lasagna noodles
  • 15–16 oz (425–450 g) ricotta or cottage cheese
  • 2 cups shredded mozzarella cheese
  • ⅓ cup grated Parmesan cheese
  • 1 teaspoon Italian seasoning
  • Salt and black pepper, to taste
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil

Instructions

Prepare the meat sauce: Heat the olive oil in a large pan over medium heat. Add the chopped onion and cook until softened.

Add the ground beef and cook until browned, breaking it apart as it cooks.

Add the garlic and cook for about 30 seconds. Stir in the tomato sauce and Italian seasoning. Season with salt and pepper.

Reduce the heat and simmer the sauce for about 15 minutes.

Cook the noodles: Cook the lasagna noodles according to the package instructions, then drain them.

Assemble the lasagna: Spread some meat sauce over the bottom of a 9 × 13-inch baking dish. Add a layer of noodles, followed by some ricotta/cottage cheese and mozzarella.

Repeat the layers until the ingredients are used, finishing with meat sauce, mozzarella, and Parmesan on top.

Bake: Bake at 350°F (175°C) for about 30 minutes, or until the cheese is melted and bubbling.

Let the lasagna rest for about 10 minutes before cutting and serving.
