Taco Recipe

These easy homemade tacos are filled with seasoned ground beef, fresh vegetables, cheese, and a flavorful combination of toppings. They're quick to prepare and perfect for a casual dinner.

Ingredients

  • 500 g ground beef
  • 8 taco shells or tortillas
  • 1 small onion, diced
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 tablespoon cooking oil
  • 1 tablespoon chili powder
  • 1 teaspoon ground cumin
  • ½ teaspoon paprika
  • Salt and black pepper, to taste
  • 1 cup shredded lettuce
  • 1 tomato, diced
  • 1 cup shredded cheddar cheese
  • Sour cream, for serving
  • Salsa, for serving

Instructions

  1. Heat the oil in a large frying pan over medium heat.
  2. Add the onion and cook until softened.
  3. Add the ground beef and cook until browned, breaking it apart as it cooks.
  4. Add the garlic, chili powder, cumin, paprika, salt, and pepper.
  5. Stir everything together and cook for another 2–3 minutes.
  6. Warm the taco shells or tortillas according to their package instructions.
  7. Fill each shell with the seasoned beef.
  8. Add lettuce, tomato, and shredded cheese.
  9. Top with salsa and sour cream.
  10. Serve immediately and enjoy!
